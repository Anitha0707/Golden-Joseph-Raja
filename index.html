About Me

A trusted advisor for families across Tamil Nadu

I am A. Golden Raja Joseph (M.A. , B.Ed, PGDEA), a Senior Insurance & Financial Advisor with the Life Insurance Corporation of India, associated with CBO 1 Branch, Madurai Division.

My approach is simple: understand your family's goals first, then recommend the plan that genuinely fits — whether it is protection for your loved ones, savings for your child's education, a regular income after retirement, or health cover for hospital emergencies.

I have been honoured by LIC's Madurai Division with the Republic Day Honour 2020, the Republic Day Award medal and the June Star Trophy, and I took part in LIC's official Guinness World Records™ title attempt for the most life insurance policies sold in 24 hours (20 January 2025).
